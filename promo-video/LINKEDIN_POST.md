I didn’t want to quit Instagram or YouTube. I wanted to stop surrendering my time to their infinite feeds.

So I built **Focus Wall** — an open-source Android app that blocks doomscrolling without cutting you off from the people you care about.

You can still:

- view Stories
- reply to DMs
- check notifications
- create posts
- watch a Reel someone intentionally shares with you
- browse normal YouTube videos

What changes is the endless loop. You choose a daily 0–60 minute budget for Reels and Shorts. The timer only runs while short-form content is actually on screen. When the budget ends, the infinite feed is blocked while the useful parts of the apps remain available.

Under the hood, this became a much deeper engineering project than a simple timer: Android Accessibility detection, screen-state classification, shared-Reel handling, cached-content edge cases, recurring daily commitments, API 36 support, privacy safeguards, CI, signed releases, and extensive real-device testing.

Focus Wall has no ads, no social-media login, no analytics, and no cloud tracking. Detection and limits stay on the device.

I’m now opening a small early-access beta for the first authorized Android devices. If doomscrolling has ever stolen “just five minutes” from you, I’d genuinely value your feedback as a tester — or a GitHub star if the idea resonates.
