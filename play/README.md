# Play Store beta submission

This folder contains the review material for Focus Wall v1.4.0. Verify every answer against the final uploaded AAB and Play Console wording before submission.

## Release order

1. Upload the signed `Focus-Wall-v1.4.0.aab` to Internal testing.
2. Use `https://abhyuday007.github.io/focus-wall/privacy.html` as the privacy-policy URL.
3. Complete App access, Ads, Content rating, Target audience, Data safety, and AccessibilityService declarations.
4. Record the real-device disclosure video described in `accessibility-declaration.md`.
5. Test installation, onboarding, consent acceptance and refusal, Instagram paths, YouTube paths, withdrawal, diagnostics clearing, update, and uninstall.
6. Promote the same reviewed build to a closed test.
7. If the developer account is subject to the new-personal-account rule, keep at least 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days before requesting production access.

## Required manual evidence

- Play Console screenshots after all App content declarations are complete.
- Public unlisted video URL for the AccessibilityService declaration.
- Closed-test tester list and feedback log.
- Final device/version matrix.
