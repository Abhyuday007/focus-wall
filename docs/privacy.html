Focus Wall

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Focus Wall is developed by Abhyuday Purohit as a local-first Android attention-management utility.

Data collection

Focus Wall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, cloud database, crash-reporting SDK, or social-media login.

Accessibility service

With explicit user consent, Focus Wall’s Accessibility service processes app and window changes, clicks, scroll events, package names, view identifiers, selected states, component class names, and scroll distance on the device. This is used only to identify supported Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts surfaces, measure time spent there, and show the user’s limit card.

Focus Wall does not read, record, collect, or share screen text, direct messages, Stories, passwords, contacts, media, search terms, account names, or social-media credentials. It does not perform gestures, press Back, change device settings, or autonomously act inside another app.

On-device storage and retention

The daily limit, onboarding state, Accessibility consent record, and up to 40 sanitized detector events are stored in the app’s private preferences. Detector events contain only time, supported app package and version, detector result, and action. They remain until the user clears them in the diagnostics screen, clears app data, or uninstalls Focus Wall. Android cloud backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled.

Network and third parties

Focus Wall requests no Internet permission. It may ask Android to open Instagram, YouTube, Android Settings, this public privacy-policy page, or the user’s email application. Those destinations operate under their own privacy policies. Focus Wall does not send them diagnostic or usage data.

User controls

Users can decline or withdraw Accessibility consent, disable the service in Android Settings, clear local diagnostics, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Focus Wall has no server-side account or data to delete.

Children

Focus Wall is a general attention-management utility and is not directed to children under 13. It is not a parental-control or medical application.

Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected in the in-app policy, this page, and the effective date above.
